Quick answers

Before you write

What's the fastest way to order?

Online, in the JOMB Store. The new menu posts every Wednesday evening; order by Saturday at 8 PM for delivery on Tuesday, 10 AM – 5 PM.

How do I ask about catering or a private dinner?

Email jomborders@gmail.com or call (919) 443-5408 with your date, guest count, location, occasion, dietary needs and budget. Chef Mike will come back with ideas for a custom menu.

Can I pick up my order in person?

Meals are delivered to your home, your office or your CrossFit gym every Tuesday, 10 AM – 5 PM. Just One More Bite doesn't have a walk-in storefront.

Do you deliver to my town?

We deliver to Raleigh, Cary, Morrisville, Research Triangle Park (RTP) and Knightdale. Somewhere else in the Triangle? Email your town and ZIP code and we'll tell you whether we can reach you.

Just One More BiteChef-made · Raleigh, NC

Just One More Bite (JOMB) is a chef-made weekly meal delivery service in Raleigh, North Carolina, run by Executive Chef Michael Saunders. Every week JOMB writes a new Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free and Family menu, cooks it from scratch in Raleigh, and delivers it on Tuesday to homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. JOMB also offers protein by the pound, private chef dinners, catering and weddings.
