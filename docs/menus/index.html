Reading the menu

Menu questions

When does the new menu come out?

Chef Mike posts brand-new menus every Wednesday evening. Orders for the following Tuesday close Saturday at 8 PM, and meals are delivered Tuesday between 10 AM – 5 PM.

What does “2 of each” mean on the menu?

It's how many servings of each dish you get. Every course adds up to six meals for one person: three dishes with two of each, or, for Zone breakfasts, two dishes with three of each. A full Zone week is 6 breakfasts, 6 lunches and 6 dinners (18 meals).

Can I swap a dish I don't like?

Meals are cooked exactly as listed, with no substitutions. That's how Chef Mike keeps quality high and prices fair. Every menu is new each week, so next week brings different dishes.

Do I have to order the weekly menus every week?

No. Order any single week you like in the JOMB Store, with no contract. If you'd rather have meals every week, a JOMB meal plan saves you up to 12% and lets you skip any week.

What if I have a food allergy?

Gluten-free and dairy-free meals are made in a kitchen that also handles gluten and dairy. If you have a severe allergy, email jomborders@gmail.com before you order so we can talk it through.
