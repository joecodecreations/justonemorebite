Membership questions

How the membership works

What is the JOMB Membership?

It's a weekly meal plan subscription from Just One More Bite in Raleigh, NC. You choose who's eating (just you, two of you, or a family of up to six), how many days a week (2, 3, 4, 5 or 7) and which meals. Chef Michael Saunders cooks your plan from scratch every week and delivers it on Tuesday. Members pay less than one-off orders, keep their price locked in, and can skip or pause any week. Build your plan to see your exact weekly price.

How does billing work?

Your plan is billed once a week at your subscriber price, for the week ahead, after the Saturday at 8 PM cutoff. You only pay for the weeks you get, so skipped or paused weeks are never charged. Your subscriber price stays locked for as long as you stay subscribed. Prices are before NC sales tax.

Can I skip or pause a week?

Yes, any week. Email jomborders@gmail.com or call (919) 443-5408 by Saturday at 8 PM, and that Tuesday's delivery is skipped and not charged. Going away for a month? Pause for as long as you need. Your subscriber price is waiting when you come back.

Can I change my plan?

Anytime. Switch menus, add or drop days, go from dinners to lunch and dinner, or move from a plan for two to a family plan. Changes made by Saturday at 8 PM start with the next Tuesday delivery, and your price simply follows the plan you're on.

When are meals delivered?

Every Tuesday, 10 AM – 5 PM, to your home, office or CrossFit gym across Raleigh, Cary, Morrisville, Research Triangle Park (RTP) and Knightdale. Plans of 5 and 7 days a week get a second delivery on Friday, so the end of your week is just as fresh as the start. See the delivery area.

How do 5- and 7-day plans work with a weekly menu?

Members on bigger plans get every dish on the week's menu, plus Chef Mike's pick of repeat favorites to fill the extra days. It's all still cooked fresh, and it's never the same week twice, because the menu is brand-new every Wednesday evening. See the latest menus.

What if I'm away or not home on delivery day?

Traveling? Skip that week by Saturday at 8 PM and you won't be charged. Just out for the day? Leave a cooler with ice packs and our driver will tuck your meals inside, or have your plan delivered to your office or gym instead.

How big are the family portions?

One family dinner feeds three adults, or two adults and two small kids. The Family of 3–4 plan brings one family dinner a night, and the Big family plan brings two, enough for up to six. Want more on the table? Add the soup of the week ($20), an entrée salad ($35) or fresh-cut fruit ($35) in the JOMB Store any week. See family meal delivery.

Can I mix menus?

Yes. Each person on a plan can choose their own menu, so one of you can eat Zone while the other eats Low-Carb Dairy-Free. You can also combine plans, like family dinners for the house plus Zone lunches for the athlete. Each part gets its subscriber price. Just mention it when you start your plan.

Is there a contract?

No. There's no contract and no minimum term. Cancel anytime before the Saturday at 8 PM cutoff, and nothing more is charged. Most members stay because dinner is one less thing to think about, not because they have to.

How do I start a membership?

Build your plan on this page and tap Start this plan. An email to Chef Mike opens with your plan already filled in. Add your name, delivery address, phone, any allergies and the week you'd like to start, then hit send. Prefer to talk it through? Call (919) 443-5408.
