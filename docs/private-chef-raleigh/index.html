Good questions

Private dinners, answered

What is a private chef dinner with Just One More Bite?

Executive Chef Michael Saunders cooks a plated, restaurant-style dinner in your home for your occasion: a birthday, an anniversary, a holiday dinner or a milestone party. The menu is written for your guests, the season and any dietary needs. Before Just One More Bite, Chef Mike was Executive Sous Chef at Morton's The Steakhouse in Dallas and Executive Chef at The Ore House and at Oryx in Raleigh.

How much does a private chef dinner cost in Raleigh?

Every dinner is quoted for the occasion, and there are no fixed packages. Tell us your budget along with your date, guest count and the kind of meal you have in mind, and Chef Mike will design the menu around it. Email jomborders@gmail.com or call (919) 443-5408.

Where does Chef Mike cook private dinners?

In your home, in Raleigh and the surrounding Triangle. Tell us your town when you inquire. If you're farther out, ask: we'll tell you honestly whether we can make it work.

Can the menu work around allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. List every allergy and dietary need when you inquire, and the menu is written around them. Chef Mike cooks gluten-free, dairy-free, Paleo and low-carb food every week for JOMB's meal plans, so a restricted guest still gets a real plate.

How many guests can a private dinner serve?

Private dinners are plated, restaurant-style, so they suit a dinner-party table. For a bigger crowd, Chef Mike also caters with buffet, plated and cocktail hors d'oeuvres service; see catering & weddings. Tell us your guest count and he'll recommend the right format.

Can Chef Mike cook our Thanksgiving or holiday dinner?

Holiday dinners are one of the occasions Chef Mike cooks for, and he's booking holiday dinners and events now. Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, 2026. Send your date, guest count and town as soon as you know them.
