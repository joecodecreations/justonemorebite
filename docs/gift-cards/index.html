Good questions

Gifting, answered

How much are Just One More Bite gift cards?

Gift cards start at $50 and are sold in the JOMB Store, where every weekly menu is ordered.

Can I send a week of meals to someone else?

Yes. Order their meals in the JOMB Store and enter the recipient's name and delivery address at checkout, or email jomborders@gmail.com with their details. Meals arrive Tuesday, 10 AM – 5 PM.

Where can gift meals be delivered?

Across the Raleigh area: Raleigh, Cary, Morrisville, Research Triangle Park (RTP) and Knightdale. Somewhere else in the Triangle? Ask before you order and we'll tell you if we can reach them.

What if they aren't home on delivery day?

Ask them to leave a cooler outside with ice packs, and our driver will put the meals inside. A 48-quart cooler holds most orders.

What's a good gift for new parents?

Family dinners. Each one feeds three adults, or two adults and two small children, at $75 a dinner with a two-dinner minimum. They heat it, plate it and eat a real dinner without cooking.

Chef-made meals · Raleigh, NC

Just One More Bite (JOMB) is a chef-made weekly meal delivery service in Raleigh, North Carolina, run by Executive Chef Michael Saunders. Every week JOMB writes a new Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free and Family menu, cooks it from scratch in Raleigh, and delivers it on Tuesday to homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. JOMB also offers protein by the pound, private chef dinners, catering and weddings.
