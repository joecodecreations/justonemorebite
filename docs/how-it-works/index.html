Your week, start to finish

One order. Six days of real food.

The rhythm never changes, so it fits around your life. Here's exactly what happens between the new menu and your first bite.

  1. Wednesday evening

    The new menu posts

    Chef Mike writes brand-new Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free and Family menus every week. They go up on the menu page and in the JOMB Store.

  2. Until Sat 8 PM

    You order online

    Pick a full week of Zone meals, just lunches, a few Family dinners or protein by the pound, and pay securely in the JOMB Store. On a weekly plan? Your week is already set.

  3. Saturday at 8 PM

    Orders close

    That's the cutoff for next Tuesday. Missed it? The next menu goes up Wednesday evening.

  4. After the cutoff

    Chef Mike cooks

    Chef Mike shops and cooks your week from scratch in Raleigh, with USDA Choice meats and local produce when possible, then portions and packs every meal.

  5. Tuesday · 10 AM – 5 PM

    Your week arrives

    Delivered to your home, office or gym. Heat, plate, eat. No shopping, no prep and no dishes until next Tuesday.

Ordering questions

How it works, answered

How does Just One More Bite work?

Chef Michael Saunders posts a brand-new menu every Wednesday evening. You order online in the JOMB Store by Saturday at 8 PM, the kitchen cooks everything from scratch in Raleigh, and your meals are delivered on Tuesday between 10 AM – 5 PM to your home, office or CrossFit gym.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across the Raleigh area, including Raleigh, Cary, Morrisville, Research Triangle Park (RTP) and Knightdale. Meals can go to your home, your workplace or your CrossFit gym. Somewhere else in the Triangle? Call or email us at (919) 443-5408 / jomborders@gmail.com and we'll tell you whether we can reach you.

When is the order deadline?

Orders for the next Tuesday close Saturday at 8 PM. The new menu goes up every Wednesday evening, so you have a few days to choose.

What if I'm not home on delivery day?

No problem. Leave a cooler outside with ice packs and our driver will transfer your meals into it. A 48-quart cooler is plenty for most orders. If you pick up at a gym or office drop point, meals arrive in JOMB cooler bags, and those go back to the drop point before the next delivery.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. You can order any single week, no contract. If you'd like dinner handled every week, the JOMB Membership plans save 5–12% and you can skip or pause any week by Saturday at 8 PM. Chef Mike recommends at least 3–4 weeks in a row on the Low-Carb Dairy-Free plan to really feel the difference.

How do I pay?

Checkout is online and secure in the JOMB Store. Prices are listed before sales tax.

What do I get in a week of Zone meals?

The full Zone plan is 18 meals for one person: 6 breakfasts (two dishes, 3 of each), 6 lunches (three dishes, 2 of each) and 6 dinners (three dishes, 2 of each). It costs $253 before tax, or $14.05 a meal. You can also order just the breakfasts, lunches or dinners.

What do I get on the Paleo or Low-Carb Dairy-Free menu?

Each menu is sold as 6 lunches and/or 6 dinners for one person: three dishes, 2 of each. Low-Carb Dairy-Free is $75 for the lunches and $108 for the dinners. Paleo is $87 and $117. Double portions of Low-Carb Dairy-Free are available for two; just ask.

Can I order from more than one menu in the same week?

Yes. Each plan is its own item in the JOMB Store, so you can put more than one in your cart. For example, Zone lunches for you and two Family dinners for the house, all delivered together on Tuesday.

Can meals be delivered to my office?

Yes. We deliver to workplaces as well as homes, including offices in Research Triangle Park (RTP). Shared office and gym drop points get JOMB cooler bags, which go back to the same drop point before the next delivery. Call (919) 443-5408 to set one up.

Can I pick up my meals at my CrossFit gym?

Yes. JOMB delivers to CrossFit gyms on Tuesday, and your meals wait there in JOMB cooler bags. Return the bags to the gym before the next delivery. Ask us which gyms are on the route, or whether yours can be added.

Can I send meals to someone else?

Yes. Order as usual and send us their delivery address, and Chef Mike's meals arrive at their door on Tuesday. Clients have done this for an aging parent and for family going through a hard stretch. Gift cards start at $50 in the JOMB Store.

Who cooks the food?

Executive Chef Michael Saunders. Chef Mike was Executive Sous Chef at Morton's The Steakhouse in Dallas and Executive Chef at The Ore House in Steamboat Springs and at Oryx in Raleigh before starting Just One More Bite.

What ingredients do you use?

Everything is made from scratch, with local and fresh ingredients whenever possible and USDA Choice meats. The menu changes every week, so you're never stuck in a rotation.

Can I make substitutions?

Meals are cooked exactly as listed on the weekly menu, with no substitutions. That's how we keep quality high and prices fair.

What about food allergies?

Our gluten-free and dairy-free meals are made in a kitchen that also handles gluten and dairy. If you have a severe allergy, email jomborders@gmail.com before ordering so we can talk it through.

Do you provide nutrition information?

The Zone menu is built to 4-block targets: about 28–32 g protein, 34–40 g carbohydrates and 12–16 g fat per meal. Because every menu is new each week, we don't publish full nutrition labels for the other menus, and the Zone numbers are targets rather than lab-tested values.
