The facts

Everything about JOMB, on one page.

Who we are, where and when we deliver, what it costs and how to order. If a detail changes, it changes here first.

Business
Just One More Bite (JOMB). Legal name: Just One More Bite, LLC.
What it is
A weekly chef-made meal delivery service. Meals arrive fully cooked (it isn't a meal kit): you heat, plate and eat. Also private chef dinners, catering and weddings.
Chef & owner
Chef Michael Saunders, Executive Chef & Owner. 20+ years in professional kitchens: Executive Sous Chef at Morton's The Steakhouse (Dallas, TX), Executive Chef at The Ore House (Steamboat Springs, CO) and Executive Chef at Oryx (Raleigh, NC).
Based in
Raleigh, North Carolina. Every meal is cooked from scratch in our Raleigh kitchen.
Delivery area
Raleigh, Cary, Morrisville, Research Triangle Park (RTP) and Knightdale, NC. Elsewhere in the Triangle? Ask. See the delivery area.
Delivered to
Homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. Not home? Leave a cooler with ice packs (48 quarts is plenty for most orders). Gym and office drop points use JOMB cooler bags, returned to the drop point each week.
Delivery day
Tuesday, 10 AM – 5 PM, every week. The larger 5- and 7-day membership plans add a second delivery on Friday.
Order deadline
Saturday at 8 PM for the following Tuesday.
Menu schedule
A brand-new menu every Wednesday evening. No fixed rotation.
Menus
  • Zone: breakfasts, lunches and dinners built to 4-block targets, about 28–32 g protein, 34–40 g carbs and 12–16 g fat per meal
  • Paleo: lunches and dinners with no grains, no dairy and no added sugar
  • Low-Carb Dairy-Free: lunches and dinners for lighter, cleaner eating
  • Family dinners: each feeds three adults, or two adults and two small children
  • Protein by the pound: the week's cooked chicken, CAB beef, pork and seafood
Prices
  • Zone, all 18 meals: $253 ($14.05 a meal); double for two, 36 meals: $486 ($13.50 a meal)
  • Zone, 6 meals: breakfasts $73.50, lunches $81, dinners $108
  • Low-Carb Dairy-Free, 6 meals: lunches $75, dinners $108
  • Paleo, 6 meals: lunches $87, dinners $117
  • Family dinner: $75 each, two-dinner minimum; soup $20, entrée salad $35, fresh-cut fruit $35
  • Cooked protein per pound: chicken $10.99, CAB beef $13.99, pork $14.99, seafood $18.99 ($100 minimum on its own)
  • Gift cards from $50
All prices before NC sales tax. Per-meal math and best-value plans.
Commitment
No contract. Order week by week, or join JOMB Membership, weekly meal plans that save 5–12% on one-off prices, with skip or pause any week.
Ingredients
Made from scratch with USDA Choice meats and local, fresh ingredients when possible. Meals are cooked as listed, with no substitutions.
Allergies
Gluten-free and dairy-free meals are made in a kitchen that also handles gluten and dairy. With a severe allergy, email jomborders@gmail.com before ordering.

The basics

JOMB in four questions

What is Just One More Bite (JOMB)?

Just One More Bite (JOMB) is a chef-made weekly meal delivery service in Raleigh, North Carolina, run by Executive Chef Michael Saunders. Every week JOMB writes a new Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free and Family menu, cooks it from scratch in Raleigh, and delivers it on Tuesday to homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. JOMB also offers protein by the pound, private chef dinners, catering and weddings.

Who owns Just One More Bite?

Chef Michael Saunders, Executive Chef and owner, runs Just One More Bite. The company's legal name is Just One More Bite, LLC, based in Raleigh, North Carolina.

Is Just One More Bite a meal kit?

No. JOMB meals arrive fully cooked, so there's nothing to chop or cook. You heat, plate and eat. The menu is new every week and written by an executive chef.

How do I order from Just One More Bite?

Order online in the JOMB Store by Saturday at 8 PM for delivery the following Tuesday, 10 AM – 5 PM. Questions? Call (919) 443-5408 or email jomborders@gmail.com.
