Price questions

What it costs, answered

How much does meal prep cost in Raleigh with Just One More Bite?

Individual meals cost $12.25 to $19.50 each before tax, depending on the menu and the meal. The full Zone plan is $253 for 18 meals ($14.05 a meal), and a Family dinner is $75 and feeds three adults. Every price is on the pricing page.

What is the cheapest way to order?

Per meal, Zone breakfasts ($12.25) and Low-Carb Dairy-Free lunches ($12.50) are the lowest prices. For a whole week of eating, the full Zone plan is the best value: 18 meals at $14.05 each, or $13.50 each when you order the double portion for two.

Is the full Zone plan cheaper than buying meals separately?

Yes. Bought separately, 6 Zone breakfasts, lunches and dinners come to $262.50. The full plan is $253, which saves $9.50 a week. The double portion for two is $486, $20 less than two single plans.

How much is a family dinner per person?

A Family dinner is $75 and feeds three adults, so it works out to $25 per adult. Fed to two adults and two small children, it's $18.75 a person. There's a two-meal minimum.

Do prices include sales tax?

No. Every price on this site is before North Carolina sales tax, which is added at checkout in the JOMB Store. You see your full total before you pay.

Is there a minimum order at Just One More Bite?

Meal plans are sold as sets of 6 (6 breakfasts, 6 lunches or 6 dinners). Family dinners have a two-meal minimum. Cooked protein by the pound has a $100 minimum when you order it on its own, or you can add it to any meal order.

How much does Just One More Bite cost?

Zone meals run $14.05 per meal on the full plan ($253 for 18 meals), or $13.50 per meal for two people. Low-Carb Dairy-Free lunches are $12.50 and dinners $18.00; Paleo lunches are $14.50 and dinners $19.50. Family dinners are $75 each and feed three adults. All prices are before tax. See the full price list.

Which menu should I pick?

Zone if you train hard or want fat loss with lean muscle (breakfast, lunch and dinner, macro-balanced). Paleo if you avoid grains, dairy and added sugar. Low-Carb Dairy-Free for cleaner, lighter eating. Family for real dinners the whole house will eat.

How many people does a family meal feed?

One family dinner feeds three adults, or two adults and two small children. There's a two-meal minimum. The soup, entrée salad and vegetarian options are a little smaller and usually serve two adults.

Can two people share a plan?

Yes. The Zone plan comes in a double portion ($486 for 36 meals, $13.50 each), and double portions of the Low-Carb Dairy-Free plan are available too; just ask.
