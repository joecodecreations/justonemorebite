Protein questions

By the pound, explained

What does “cooked weight” mean?

The protein is weighed after it's cooked, so the pound you pay for is the pound you eat.

Is there a minimum order?

Add protein by the pound to any meal order, or order protein on its own with a $100 minimum before tax.

Which proteins can I buy?

The cooked proteins from that week's menus, so the selection changes weekly. Prices before tax: chicken $10.99/lb, CAB beef $13.99/lb, pork $14.99/lb and seafood $18.99/lb.

When do I order, and when does it arrive?

Order by Saturday at 8 PM. Your protein is delivered with the week's meals on Tuesday, 10 AM – 5 PM, anywhere in Raleigh, Cary, Morrisville, Research Triangle Park (RTP) and Knightdale.

Can I add protein to my Zone or family order?

Yes. Protein by the pound works as an add-on to any Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free or Family order. Add it to your cart in the JOMB Store.

Just One More BiteChef-made · Raleigh, NC

Just One More Bite (JOMB) is a chef-made weekly meal delivery service in Raleigh, North Carolina, run by Executive Chef Michael Saunders. Every week JOMB writes a new Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free and Family menu, cooks it from scratch in Raleigh, and delivers it on Tuesday to homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. JOMB also offers protein by the pound, private chef dinners, catering and weddings.
