About these reviews

Are these Just One More Bite reviews from real clients?

Yes. Every testimonial on this page was written by a real client or colleague of Chef Michael Saunders and first appeared on JOMB's original website. We show first names and last initials.

Where can I leave a review for Just One More Bite?

On Google (search "Just One More Bite Raleigh NC" and choose Write a review) or on JOMB's Facebook page. We ask every client the same way and never offer anything in return for a review.

Will I get the same results as the clients on this page?

Individual results vary. JOMB meals are chef-made food that supports your goals; they aren't medical treatment. Talk with your doctor about any medical diet.

Chef-made meals · Raleigh, NC

Just One More Bite (JOMB) is a chef-made weekly meal delivery service in Raleigh, North Carolina, run by Executive Chef Michael Saunders. Every week JOMB writes a new Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free and Family menu, cooks it from scratch in Raleigh, and delivers it on Tuesday to homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. JOMB also offers protein by the pound, private chef dinners, catering and weddings.
