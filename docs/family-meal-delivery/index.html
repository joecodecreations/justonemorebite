Family dinner questions

Before you order

How many people does a family dinner feed?

One family dinner feeds three adults, or two adults and two small children. The soup, entrée salad and vegetarian options are a little smaller and usually serve two adults.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes: two family dinners per order. Each dinner is $75 before tax.

What can I add to a family order?

A soup of the week ($20), an entrée salad ($35) and a 1-gallon bag of fresh-cut fruit in several varieties ($35). The extras change with the menu each week.

What does a family dinner cost per person?

At $75 for three adults, it's $25.00 per adult serving, before tax, with no shopping, cooking or dishes.

Can I send family dinners as a gift?

Yes. Gift cards start at $50, or you can send a week of dinners straight to someone else's door in our delivery area. See gift cards.

What if we're not home on Tuesday?

Leave a cooler outside with ice packs and the driver will move your dinners into it. A 48-quart cooler is plenty for most orders.

Chef-made meals · Raleigh, NC

Just One More Bite (JOMB) is a chef-made weekly meal delivery service in Raleigh, North Carolina, run by Executive Chef Michael Saunders. Every week JOMB writes a new Zone, Paleo, Low-Carb Dairy-Free and Family menu, cooks it from scratch in Raleigh, and delivers it on Tuesday to homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. JOMB also offers protein by the pound, private chef dinners, catering and weddings.
