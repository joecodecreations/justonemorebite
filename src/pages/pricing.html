Get the Zone double: {{price:zone_double}} for 36 meals, {{per:zone_double}} each, and $20 less than two single plans. Double portions of Low-Carb Dairy-Free are available too; just ask.

Best for households

“I'm feeding a family.”

Family dinners are {{price:family_meal}} each and feed three adults ($25 each) or two adults and two small kids ($18.75 each). The two-dinner minimum is $150. Add a soup ({{price:family_soup}}) or entrée salad ({{price:family_salad}}) to stretch a night.

Best for workdays

“I just need lunches.”

Six lunches cost {{price:lcdf_lunch}} on Low-Carb Dairy-Free ({{per:lcdf_lunch}} each), {{price:zone_lunch}} on Zone ({{per:zone_lunch}}) or {{price:paleo_lunch}} on Paleo ({{per:paleo_lunch}}). We deliver to offices, too.

Most chef time per plate

“I only want dinners.”

Six dinners are {{price:zone_dinner}} on Zone or Low-Carb Dairy-Free ({{per:zone_dinner}} each) and {{price:paleo_dinner}} on Paleo ({{per:paleo_dinner}}). Dinner is the priciest meal to buy on its own; add lunches and your average drops.
