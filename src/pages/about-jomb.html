The facts

Everything about JOMB, on one page.

Who we are, where and when we deliver, what it costs and how to order. If a detail changes, it changes here first.

Business
Just One More Bite (JOMB). Legal name: Just One More Bite, LLC.
What it is
A weekly chef-made meal delivery service. Meals arrive fully cooked (it isn't a meal kit): you heat, plate and eat. Also private chef dinners, catering and weddings.
Chef & owner
{{chef}}, Executive Chef & Owner. {{chef_years}} years in professional kitchens: Executive Sous Chef at Morton's The Steakhouse (Dallas, TX), Executive Chef at The Ore House (Steamboat Springs, CO) and Executive Chef at Oryx (Raleigh, NC).
Based in
Raleigh, North Carolina. Every meal is cooked from scratch in our Raleigh kitchen. {{trust}}
Delivery area
{{towns_sentence}}, NC. Elsewhere in the Triangle? Ask. See the delivery area.
Delivered to
Homes, offices and CrossFit gyms. Not home? Leave a cooler with ice packs (48 quarts is plenty for most orders). Gym and office drop points use JOMB cooler bags, returned to the drop point each week.
Delivery day
{{delivery_day}}, {{delivery_window}}, every week. The larger 5- and 7-day membership plans add a second delivery on Friday.
Order deadline
{{cutoff}} for the following {{delivery_day}}.
Menu schedule
A brand-new menu every {{menu_posted}}. No fixed rotation.
Menus
  • Zone: breakfasts, lunches and dinners built to 4-block targets, about 28–32 g protein, 34–40 g carbs and 12–16 g fat per meal
  • Paleo: lunches and dinners with no grains, no dairy and no added sugar
  • Low-Carb Dairy-Free: lunches and dinners for lighter, cleaner eating
  • Family dinners: each feeds three adults, or two adults and two small children
  • Protein by the pound: the week's cooked chicken, CAB beef, pork and seafood

Commitment
No contract. Order week by week, or join JOMB Membership, weekly meal plans that save 5–12% on one-off prices, with skip or pause any week.
Ingredients
Made from scratch with USDA Choice meats and local, fresh ingredients when possible. Meals are cooked as listed, with no substitutions.
Allergies
Gluten-free and dairy-free meals are made in a kitchen that also handles gluten and dairy. With a severe allergy, email {{email_link}} before ordering.
